# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Software engineers, platform/SRE folks, tech leads, and adjacent builders who discover and rotate through small utilities during real work: prep for interviews, sketch strategy, edit JSON, run drills, ship slides. They land on Neorgon when they want a **single, trustworthy index** of focused tools instead of hunting scattered bookmarks or noisy marketplaces.

## Product Purpose

Neorgon.com is the **canonical hub** for a constellation of AI-assisted, open, practical utilities. It groups every Neorgon surface in one place so tech workers can **scan, compare, and jump** to the right tool quickly. Success is: fewer wrong clicks, faster “oh, that exists” moments, and a brand that reads as **capable and generous**, not extractive.

## Brand Personality

**Voice:** direct, slightly irreverent, builder-to-builder. Confident without chest-beating.

**Three words:** capable, generous, electric.

**The position:** custom tailored tools for fast iterations and modern work. The
tempo is the differentiator and it is measurable: 51 usable tools, the oldest six
months old, 17 added in the last 30 days. State it as pace, never as
incompleteness. See `docs/reviews/neorgon-site/2026-08-28-brand-perception-audit.md`
in the private root repo.

**Emotional goals:** relief (finally, one sane index), curiosity (what else is here?), and quiet pride in shipping useful things without a paywall or signup wall.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing patterns that bury the catalog: hero metrics, endless identical icon cards, modal newsletter traps.
- “AI wrapper” aesthetics that feel interchangeable: purple gradients on white, stock illustrations, vague superlatives.
- Dark patterns: fake urgency, signup gates on read-only discovery, disguised ads as tools.
- Corporate “trust center” blandness that strips all character from engineering brands.

## Design Principles

1. **Catalog clarity beats spectacle:** motion and atmosphere support wayfinding; they never replace scannable structure, tags, and predictable card anatomy.
2. **Consolidate, then route:** the hub’s job is triage and deep links. Each destination owns its own UX; the hub stays fast and legible.
3. **Practice the promise:** if the story is “tools for people who ship,” the page itself loads quickly, respects reduced motion, and exposes a skip link to the tool grid.
4. **Earned personality:** humor and Easter eggs are optional seasoning for people who explore; the primary path stays obvious for first-time visitors.
5. **Honest about craft:** AI-assisted development is part of the story only insofar as it helps users trust velocity and iteration, not as hype or a substitute for usefulness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA** for core navigation, focus order, contrast on links and tags, and visible focus states. Honor **`prefers-reduced-motion`** for ambient animation (starfield and decorative layers). Keep icon-only controls paired with `aria-label` or visible text where space allows. Avoid conveying meaning by color alone for status or categories; pair with text or shape.
