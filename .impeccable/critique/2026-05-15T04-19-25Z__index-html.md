## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated?**

**LLM assessment:** No immediate "default SaaS landing" read. The page commits to a specific world (dark space grid, per-card neon, constellation hero) and copy has a clear voice ("forgot to add a paywall"). Risk zone: the grid is still many same-shaped cards with icon plus heading plus body plus chips, which matches what PRODUCT.md warns against if the accents and copy ever flatten. Today, varied `--card-accent` and featured Pathfinder placement keep it out of pure template territory.

**Deterministic scan (`npx impeccable detect --json index.html`):** Exit 2 with multiple `low-contrast` hits and one `wide-tracking` warning, plus **skipped-heading** (`<h1>` in hero then first section heading is `<h3>` "Planning", no `<h2>`).

**False positives to treat carefully:** Most `low-contrast` rows cite text on `#ffffff`, which does not match the shipped dark theme (`--bg: #000912`, `--text-primary: #f0f4ff` in `css/style.css`). The CLI is not seeing computed paint over the real background. **Do not** recolor the UI from those rows alone; run a real contrast check (browser DevTools or a paint-aware audit) on the live dark page.

**Trust the detector on:** Heading level skip (document outline for SR users). Wide tracking: confirm which selector triggered `0.15em` on body-sized text; if it is only badges or micro-labels, document that in `ignore.md` or narrow the rule in CSS.

**Visual overlays:** Not run (no browser automation in this session). For overlay validation, serve the site and inject per `reference/critique.md` when you want the `[Human]` pass.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Skipped heading level**

- **Why it matters:** Screen reader users navigate by heading levels; jumping from `h1` ("Made for…") to `h3` group labels ("Planning") breaks a predictable outline and fails the detector's skipped-heading rule.
- **Fix:** Promote group labels to `h2` (keep visual class), or insert a visually hidden `h2` for the tools region (e.g. "Tools by category") between hero and first group.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish` (structure and a11y pass on `index.html`).

**[P1] Duplicate Google tag (gtag) blocks**

- **Why it matters:** Two identical `gtag/js` loads and config blocks in `<head>` add weight, duplicate network work, and confuse anyone auditing the page.
- **Fix:** Remove the second block (lines 37-44 duplicate of 5-11).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish` or a small manual edit.

**[P2] Card grid sameness risk**

- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT anti-references call out endless identical icon cards. You are close to that shape at scale even with good copy.
- **Fix:** Vary density (featured row, compact row, or section intros) so not every row reads as the same widget factory. Keep one strong featured treatment per section if you add more tools.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout` or `/impeccable distill` depending on whether you expand or simplify.

**[P2] Verify contrast and letter-spacing on real paint**

- **Why it matters:** WCAG AA is a stated target in PRODUCT.md. CLI warnings on white backgrounds are unreliable here; you still owe a pass on the rendered dark stack (especially `.card-tag`, muted lines, and any text over glass surfaces).
- **Fix:** Manual spot-check with DevTools contrast picker; tune tokens if anything falls under 4.5:1 for body or 3:1 for large text.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit` (technical a11y pass).

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Impatient power user)**

- Duplicate `gtag` loads slow first paint slightly; Alex notices extra main-thread work before interaction.
- Keyboard: `/` hint is good; confirm focus moves into search reliably and that tab order through dozens of cards does not feel endless without landmarks (landmarks help after heading fix).

**Jordan (Confused first-timer)**

- No short inline explanation of what "Internal" vs public tools means beyond the heading; Jordan may hesitate on Rush Q or Energon HQ.
- Easter egg layers (NERV overlay, warp toasts) are fine if they never block the first path; confirm they stay inert until invoked so Jordan is not startled on load.

**Sam (Accessibility-dependent)**

- **Heading skip** is the concrete failure: after `h1`, the next heading should not be `h3`.
- Many interactive targets are full-card links; ensure one logical tab stop per card (native `<a>` wrapping is good) and visible `:focus-visible` styles that work on the glass treatment.
